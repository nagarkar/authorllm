# House rules for anything written for the author

A copy of the author's standing rules, kept in the repo so a cloud session can read them. The author's Mac copy in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` governs where the two differ; report a difference rather than resolving it.

## Vocabulary

- Never the words "refuse", "refuses", "refusal" in anything written for the author: messages, papers, prompts, reports. Say what a thing leaves alone or will not do: "declines", "leaves alone", "does not touch", "is out of bounds"; "boundaries" for the section that lists what a thing does not touch. (Ruled 2026-09-06.)
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a comma, a colon, a full stop, or parentheses.
- Capitalised "Nothing" is a name, never a pronoun. "No push moves it", not "Nothing moves it".
- A Field has a "nature", never a "habit".
- "Persistence" for the continuation of a locus's last act, never "echo", except inside a quotation from the book.
- "Gradient" is always the gradient of a potential, or the derivative of a named total; never a loose slope.
- A term the book lacks is marked "this series' term" at first use.
- Citations to the book as (Metaphysic, page) or (Sermon One, page), never a bare number.

## Form

- Every file pointed to is a markdown link whose target is the absolute path. A Doc gets its full URL.
- Every message that deals with a manuscript names it with its Google Doc link.
- Every response ends with the date and time on its own line, from `date '+%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S %Z'`.
- Nothing is committed, pushed or published on the author's behalf without the author's word.
- Abstracts are 200 words or fewer. Sentences of about twenty words, one idea each.
