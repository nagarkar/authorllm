Review a concept conflict

A graph proposal is not a prose edit. It is a claim that settled conceptual law should change. Your job is to decide whether the new note defines the concept better than the current one.

Mission: improve SMSTTD deliberatelyLive snapshot: 20 Aug 2026

The one idea

Prefer ontology over narrative role. A concept note should say what the concept is. If a proposal only says where the concept appears in the argument or what it causes later, it is usually weaker than a real definition.

The worked example: “Nothing”

SourceTextWhat it is doing
Settled graph note“The sum of all opposites, paradoxes, possibilities, and potentials, where every element is countered by its opposite, and Nothing prevails.”Defines the concept.
Dismissed proposal pr-58e88“The starting point of the sermons' metaphysics, from which the Realm of Fields and Field of Choice arise.”Describes the concept's role in the book.
Manuscript evidencesermons.md and metaphysic.md both define Nothing in ontological terms: clasp, opposites, paradoxes, neither being nor non-being.Supports the settled note.

The proposal was therefore dismissed, not because it was false, but because it was inferior as law. It flattened a definition into sequencing.

The CLI loop

  1. List the open proposals: authorlm proposal list -m SMSTTD
  2. Inspect the settled concept note: authorlm concept show "Nothing" -m SMSTTD
  3. Inspect manuscript support with a text search.
  4. Record the verdict: authorlm proposal dismiss ... --why "..." or authorlm proposal accept ...
