Choose the lane before the verb

AuthorLM is not one editing workflow. It is several evidence-bearing state machines. Your first job is to identify which one owns the decision.

Mission: improve SMSTTD deliberatelyLive snapshot: 20 Aug 2026

The one idea

The type and state of the judgment choose the tool. A critic's claim must be triaged. A graph conflict must be adjudicated. A prose goal needs an intent. A stale image needs an illustration decision. Treating them all as “edit the manuscript” destroys the evidence AuthorLM is meant to learn from.

Choose our first SMSTTD loop

Reply in chat with the letter and one sentence explaining why. That explanation is retrieval practice now—and later, when you make a verdict, it becomes the valuable evidence AuthorLM learns from.

  1. Resolve a policy question — decide whether a validated rule applies in a disputed scope.
  2. Review a concept conflict — inspect proposal pr-58e88 about “Nothing,” then adopt or dismiss it.
  3. Start a prose intent — narrow the active “Improve recapitulation” work into one session-sized goal.
  4. Refresh a stale illustration — inspect the Basilides prompt and evolve its approved composition.

Recommended first: B. It is small, reversible until your verdict, and teaches the boundary between manuscript observation and conceptual law.
