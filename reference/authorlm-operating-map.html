AuthorLM operating map

Choose the lane from the kind of judgment. Observation tells you what exists; a verdict teaches AuthorLM what the author believes.

The invariant

Critic input, model analysis, and extracted graph claims are proposals—not authorial law. The author's verdict is the boundary that changes state and creates evidence.

A compact loop

Observe→Name the lane→Inspect context→Author verdict→Apply→Record evidence

For prose work, insert declare intent before guidance and collect + complete after the author finishes.
