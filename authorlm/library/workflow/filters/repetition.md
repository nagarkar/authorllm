# repetition

Purpose: nothing is said twice by accident.

- Flag: the same uncommon word twice in a paragraph, or close enough (about three paragraphs) to be heard as an echo; the same connective or opening shape on consecutive sentences or paragraphs; the same construction twice in a row; a term defined a second time in the essay; a conclusion restated after it has landed; an argument re-run that an earlier section of this essay already made; a sentence that restates the one before and adds nothing; a further restatement after an image and its one plain statement.
- Repair: cut the second saying and close the seam; where a word must go and nothing can replace it, recast the clause. An ordinary noun repeated clumsily may be varied with a natural alternative, so long as the variant cannot be read as a different thing. A key term is never varied, only cut or recast. Where a whole paragraph is the restatement, ask whether to cut it.
- A second definition goes; a reminder of a few words after a long gap stays.

Not a finding: the techniques in the shared rules; a motif word recurring across the essay (judge the paragraph); two distinct terms that look alike; function words; a one-clause recall that orients; an image followed by one plain statement of its lesson; a story's outcome followed by its moral.

Leaves: whether a gloss is needed at all (`plain-reader`); repetition that serves the ear (`audio-friendly` never adds or removes repetition).
