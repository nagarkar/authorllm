# Mission: Operate AuthorLM as an editorial learning system

## Why
Use AuthorLM fluently to improve SMSTTD while preserving the author's judgment as the source of truth. Turn the manuscript's real critique, concept, policy, prose, Doc, and illustration queues into deliberate editorial progress rather than backlog churn.

## Success looks like
- Choose the correct AuthorLM workflow from the kind and state of an editorial problem.
- Move a real SMSTTD improvement from observation through author verdict to recorded evidence.
- Keep manuscript prose, conceptual law, external critique, and tool defects in their proper lanes.
- Resume work later from AuthorLM's stored state without reconstructing decisions from memory.

## Constraints
- The learner already knows session startup and the chat interface; lessons should not reteach setup.
- Practice on live SMSTTD work, one small decision loop at a time.
- The author makes every substantive judgment; lessons may inspect freely but must not silently mutate manuscript state.

## Out of scope
- Rebuilding AuthorLM internals unless a confirmed tool defect blocks manuscript work.
- Generic writing instruction detached from an active SMSTTD need.
